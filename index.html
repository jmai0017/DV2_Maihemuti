Melbourne After Dark

Mapping Late-Night Food Accessibility

An analysis of how food venue availability, transport access, and pedestrian activity vary across Melbourne, with a focus on late-night equity between the CBD, inner suburbs, and outer areas.

Overview: Centralised Food Landscape

Melbourne's food venues are heavily concentrated near the CBD. This report examines the spatial patterns, venue types, transport links, and late-night activity.

CBD Dominance

The central city maintains significantly higher venue density and late-night activity compared to outer suburbs.

Takeaway Reliance

Outer suburbs have fewer sit-down options and depend heavily on takeaway services after evening hours.

Transport Gaps

Many food-dense areas lack adequate late-night public transport, limiting accessibility for residents.

Spatial Inequality

Accessibility decreases rapidly beyond 5km from the CBD, highlighting urban planning challenges.

Data Sources and Methodology

City of Melbourne CLUE business dataset (2024), ABS Census data, Pedestrian Counting System, and PTV public transport data. Accessibility Index combines food venue density and transport access. All visualisations built with Vega-Lite.
